ABOUT US

Business Solutions Built Around Your Needs

General Business Solutions (GEBUSO-SARL) is a diversified business company based in Burundi, providing practical business solutions to companies, institutions, organizations, schools, and other clients.

GEBUSO operates in different areas including commercial activities, procurement and supply, consultancy, investment, research and development, training, strategic partnerships, and other business-related services.
